# The Cartographer's Ghost

Series: Impossible Maps
Pen name: A. R. Vale
Genre: Literary Mystery
Setting: contemporary urban fantasy

## Premise

A grieving cartographer discovers a hand-drawn map that redraws itself each
night, charting a city that does not exist. As she follows its streets, she
must decide whether the phantom city is a memory, a warning, or a way home.

## Themes

- Grief and the geography of memory
- The unreliability of maps and stories
- Finding home in impossible places
