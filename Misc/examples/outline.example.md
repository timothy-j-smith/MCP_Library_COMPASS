# Outline

## Chapter 1: The Inherited Desk

Mara clears out her late father's study and finds the living map tucked inside
an atlas. The ink still looks wet.

## Chapter 2: The First Phantom Street

Overnight a new avenue appears on the map. Mara walks the real city and finds
the street half-existing, flickering at the edge of sight.

## Chapter 3: Into the Impossible City

Mara steps across the boundary and into the city the map describes, where the
buildings remember her father.
